The person behind the work

I stay close
to what I ship.

I like being close enough to the code to know when a design will be difficult to build, and close enough to the user to know when it is worth the trouble.

Founding products made that practical. With Nomi, a confusing reply can be a writing problem, a data problem, or both. In enterprise work, a missing action might be a permission rule. I want to understand the whole thing before I decide what to change.

A detail I keep coming back to

What happens
when it’s wrong?

When someone tells Nomi “that’s wrong,” the useful next move is a question: is it the amount, the date, or the category?

That small interaction is the kind of work I care about. The product has to leave room for correction, and the system has to give the person a way to make it right.
